  Replay exactly one latest-wins topology action after recorder teardown. Preserve the existing

- [ ] Add deterministic policy/production-composition coverage for harmless attach during REC,
  active external removal/replacement, Starting/Recording/teardown windows, and final Ready recovery.
- [ ] **P2 / AGG10-02:** carry a definite removal/identity epoch per external ID through the ordered

Acceptance: ordinary availability noise does not reopen the camera, active REC stays Ready and
stoppable, exactly one deferred convergence runs after Stop, and a physically replaced same-ID
external camera cannot inherit stale facts.

## Phase 2 — make deleted-family intent durable, bounded, and teardown-safe

- [ ] **P3 / AGG10-03:** persist family deletion intent before acknowledgement in a recovery-visible

- [ ] Keep unresolved retry ownership process-wide or close Engine teardown behind a producer
  barrier so a late output cannot arrive after the last retry. Enforce a bounded fail-closed

- [ ] Add persistent-failure tests beyond 32 families and the full release → late output → failed

Acceptance: deletion acknowledgement has a durable veto, unresolved ownership stays bounded, and
process/Engine replacement cannot resurrect a deleted family.
